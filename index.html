ABOUT THE SPORT

What is Bloodrush?

Bloodrush is a fast-paced fantasy team sport where athletic ability meets tactical thinking. Players must work together, anticipate their opponents and make split-second decisions to gain the advantage.

THREE RULES TO LIVE BY:

  • Trust your team.
  • Know your position.
  • Never underestimate your opponent.
